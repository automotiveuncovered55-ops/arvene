ARVENE website
==============
1. Unzip this folder completely (don't open files from inside the zip viewer).
2. Double-click index.html to open the site in your browser.

Where the photos are (inside the "images" folder, in four sub-folders):
  images/campaign/      1 hero photo
  images/editorial/     1 editorial photo
  images/collections/   5 collection photos
  images/products/      11 product photos
Keep the "images" folder next to index.html, or the photos won't appear.
